Human oversight & safe handlingHuman-in-the-loop
  • Risk score prioritises outreach only; it never removes a patient from care or routine reminders.
  • A patient who says no to data use stays visible as “No data consent”. AccessBridge does not score or contact the patient; the routine clinic reminder continues.
  • Potential barriers are signals, not facts. Confirm with the patient before selecting a response.
  • Do not suggest SMS/WhatsApp when consent is absent; do not suggest phone outreach if no usable number is available without first verifying contact details.
  • Record coordinator action, outcome and patient-reported barrier; preserve an auditable history in a real system.
  • Capacity limits must be agreed with the clinic; patients beyond capacity remain visible and are not silently discarded.
  • Monitor subgroup sensitivity and no-show disparities; review the rule if patient mix or data quality changes.

A prospective, subgroup-aware validation and workflow-capacity assessment are required before any clinical deployment.
